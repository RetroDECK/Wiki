## RetroDECK 0.11.0 - What Is in It So Far?

<img src="../../../icon-rd.svg" width="75" alt=""> 

Quite a lot.

The RetroDECK Team is passionate about retro gaming culture. This update aims to showcase the wide range of people communities and projects that make up the retro gaming space.

We want to use the RetroDECK platform to tell their stories and shine a light on communities that provide significant value to retro gaming culture but may not yet be widely known.

One of RetroDECK's core missions is to make retro gaming easy and accessible:

- **Easy to play** your own game backups.
- **Easy to experience** mods, ROM hacks, fan projects and other reinterpretations of classic games.
- **Easy to explore** vintage games and platforms you may never have experienced before.
- **Easy to discover** the people and communities that continue to preserve, reinterpret and expand retro gaming culture.

RetroDECK is not only about playing old games. It is also about making the wider retro gaming culture easier to discover, experience and participate in and we want to platform to represent that by adding "oddball" systems.

This is why we are selective about which components we add to RetroDECK. 

No single persons retro gaming experience is more valuable than another. 

Retro gaming means different things to different people and RetroDECK aims to make space for all of those experiences.
