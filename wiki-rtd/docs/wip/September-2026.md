## RetroDECK Travels

With summer coming to an end, members of the RetroDECK team have been traveling and one team member recently visited a particularly unique destination: **Shenzhen, China**.

Together with a member of the Chinese KDE Team, he toured and got an inside look at several companies.

### RetroDECK Travel Log: Shenzhen

The purpose of these visits **was not** to promote RetroDECK or ask companies to ship RetroDECK on their devices. This was a fan tour with a **FOSS-focused liaison** approach, aimed at building connections, sharing ideas and encouraging better support for open source.

We talked about:

- **Firmware updates on Linux:** Many vendors hardware currently only provides firmware update tools for Windows and Android, leaving Linux users without official support. We suggested considering standardized firmware updates through **LVFS (Linux Vendor Firmware Service)** to make updates more accessible.

- **Steam Input:** We discussed how important good **Steam Input** support is and the value of testing and understanding how their devices behave with it.

- **Remapping and keyboard shortcuts:** We also talked about better remapping tools and a **keyboard hotkeys mode**. This was brought up by a KDE team member, who uses small controllers as shortcut devices when creating FOSS artwork.

- **Standardization:** We also mentioned standardization efforts and organizations such as **[Open Gaming Collective (OGC)](https://opengamingcollective.org/)**, which is working on a unified set of gaming-focused components for the Linux ecosystem.

- **Open source collaboration:** We encouraged them to work with and support open source community developers where possible, helping make their hardware and software more accessible to the wider FOSS community.

- **International support:** We discussed the importance of having strong international customer support that understands cultural differences between Chinese and Western customers and building a community.

For many of these smaller or niche Chinese companies, simply finding the right information across the barriers between East and West can be challenging.

We hope our small visits at least helped point them in the right direction and gave them some useful places to start. We cannot guarantee that anything will come from these conversations, but hopefully they were helpful in some way.

We also connected them with the Chinese KDE team member who joined us as a translator and they can shared contact details on WeChat. 

Most importantly, it was a lot of fun!

## What did your retro eyes see?!

### JSAUX / Bitmo Lab

I visited **JSAUX** and **Bitmo Lab**, where we got to see their new lineup of **GAMEBABY** iPhone-focused gaming cases with built-in controllers.

We also got to sit down, play with, and discuss a new prototype featuring a **Steam Frame E-Ink panel**. Even with a slow refresh rate, we talked about some interesting possibilities, such as:

- **Steam Achievements:** Showing achievements or other Steam events on the E-Ink panel as they happen.
- **Community plugins:** A possible "plugin store" where the community could share and upload their own creations.

**Note:** These were simply ideas shared and discussed while we were in the same room. **Talking about an idea does not mean it is planned or that anything will be implemented.** It was just a fun opportunity to exchange ideas and see what might be possible.

A big thanks to **JSAUX** and **Bitmo Lab** for hosting us and giving us the opportunity to visit, see their latest work, and exchange ideas.
