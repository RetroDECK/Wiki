## RetroDECK in the Wild

[Steam Deck Software in 2026: Checking In With The Developers Behind The Ecosystem](https://gardinerbryant.com/steam-deck-software-in-2026/)

**RetroDECK Team Comment:**

In this article, we shared our perspectives, feelings and insights alongside other developers regarding the evolution of the Steam Deck ecosystem.

[The Cemu Security PSA and Why RetroDECK’s Approach Suddenly Makes a Lot of Sense](https://gardinerbryant.com/the-cemu-security-psa-and-why-retrodecks/)

**RetroDECK Team Comment:**

Bugs, vulnerabilities and malicious actors will always exist. While we strive to mitigate risks, this does not eliminate them entirely. Risk is inherent in all systems and we remain committed doing our best to mitigate them. However, it is important to remember that this is a hobby project driven by fewer than a handful of volunteers and the situation is likely similar for Cemu. Everyone involved acts out of pure passion, which makes it particularly disheartening to witness the FOSS community being targeted and exploited in such a manner.

We have also frequently avoided critical issues by delaying the new component releases into RetroDECK, opting instead to wait for initial hotfixes. Notable examples include the Azahar emulator bug that corrupted save files and critical stability issues in a PPSSPP release. We are generally waiting one to two weeks after a major version release to allow for patches, rather than immediately consider the initial .0 version into of a tiny RetroDECK patch.

For bleeding-edge components without stable releases (RPCS3, Vita3K, etc..), the policy defaults to the latest verified "working" version.

No security method is foolproof, whether ours or other FOSS projects. While we strive to prioritize safety within our limited resources and available information, we cannot guarantee absolute immunity. Just because we have not been targeted yet, does not mean we are immune by future attacks by malicious actors.

Therefore as always, users should remain vigilant.

### Client: Satellaview+

<img src="../../../satellaview+-logo.png" width="75" alt=""> 

![type:video](satellaview_plus_tuner.mp4)

As we talked about in a previous blog post, version 0.11.0 will introduce RetroDECK's first client-type component: Mudlet. That enables users to play Multi-User Dungeon (MUD) games directly within the RetroDECK environment.

<img src="../../../client-town.png" width="500" alt=""> 
<img src="../../../client-town2.png" width="500" alt=""> 
<img src="../../../client-town3.png" width="500" alt=""> 

We are pleased to announce our second client component to include in RetroDECK in 0.11.0: Satellaview+. This client revives St. Giga's historic satellite broadcast platform, Satellaview, granting access to legacy content including exclusive games, digital magazines and new original broadcast features.

We've collaborated closely with the Satellaview+ team for quite some time to make this happen.

To access events, games, features, and more, you'll need to check their broadcasting schedule and connect at the right time.

<img src="../../../es-de-tuner.png" width="500" alt=""> 
<img src="../../../es-de-client.png" width="500" alt=""> 

The setup consists of two components: a tuner that downloads the current programming and a client that establishes the connection.

<img src="../../../client-info.png" width="500" alt=""> 
<img src="../../../client-info.png" width="500" alt=""> 

We're also hoping to organize a Satellaview+ RetroDECK event sometime after the launch of version 0.11.0, so stay "tuned".

### System: Ludusavi (evaluation) - Cloudsync

One of the most requested features in the RetroDECK backlog is a dedicated solution for cloud syncing various data. Our goal is to deliver this in version 0.11.0.

We are currently evaluating Ludusavi as the backend system to power this feature. By building custom tools around it, we aim to give users granular control over their sync preferences: from syncing save files alone to backing up your entire library or other misc data.

We won't be providing our own hosting solution. Instead, you'll have the freedom to choose from any service supported by Ludusavi ranging from major cloud providers to your own self-hosted server at home.
